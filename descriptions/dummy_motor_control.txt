About the Role

Vector Motion is developing high-performance electric drive systems for autonomous machines. We are looking for a Motor Control Firmware Engineer to develop real-time control firmware.

What You'll Do

Develop real-time embedded firmware in C and C++.
Implement motor control algorithms.
Develop high-frequency control loops.
Debug power electronics and embedded hardware.
Work with PWM peripherals and gate drivers.
Use oscilloscopes to validate motor-control performance.

Minimum Qualifications

Experience with C and C++.
Experience with embedded firmware.
Experience with real-time systems.
Experience with motor control.
Experience with PID control.
Experience with PWM.
Experience with microcontrollers.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with field-oriented control.
Experience with FOC.
Experience with BLDC motors.
Experience with PMSM motors.
Experience with gate drivers.
Experience with CAN bus.
Experience with STM32.
